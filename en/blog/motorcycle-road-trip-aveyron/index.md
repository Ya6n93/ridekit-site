The easiest way to ride Aveyron is the road trip its tourist board designed for motorcyclists: **L'Aveyron à moto, from Laguiole to Millau**, 720 km in 5 stages, crossing the whole département from the Aubrac plateau in the north to the Grands Causses in the south. RideKit's Roadbook has 14 road trips in [Aveyron](/roadbook-moto/occitanie/aveyron/) (pages in French), all published by HIT Aveyron on DATAtourisme, France's open tourism data platform.

## The 5-day road trip, stage by stage

720 km, about 12 h 35 of riding in all, and a curviness of 6.1 out of 10 (RideKit's measure of how much the road turns, from 1.5 for straight to 9.9 for hairpins). With 2 h 15 to 2 h 45 of riding a day, there is time to stop.

| Stage | Distance | Riding time | Curviness | Scenery, per HIT Aveyron |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Laguiole → Entraygues-sur-Truyère | 134 km | 2 h 20 | 5.7/10 | The Aubrac's twisty roads, then the Lot valley |
| 2. Entraygues-sur-Truyère → Najac | 147 km | 2 h 35 | 6.3/10 | Conques, the Marcillac vineyard, the bastide towns |
| 3. Najac → Rodez | 151 km | 2 h 40 | 6.6/10 | The Aveyron gorges and valley, the Ségala |
| 4. Rodez → Brousse-le-Château | 129 km | 2 h 15 | 6.4/10 | The plateau and its lakes, down to the Tarn |
| 5. Brousse-le-Château → Millau | 159 km | 2 h 45 | 5.4/10 | The Tarn valley and the Grands Causses |

Riding times are at a touring pace, without stops. Stage 3 is the twistiest. The last stage runs through Saint-Affrique, Roquefort-sur-Soulzon (yes, the cheese), the Templar town of La Couvertoirade and Nant before Millau. The Causses are part of the UNESCO World Heritage site [The Causses and the Cévennes](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1153), listed in 2011.

The full route sheet, map, stages and free GPX track: [L'Aveyron à moto, de Laguiole à Millau](/roadbook-moto/occitanie/l-aveyron-a-moto-de-laguiole-a-millau/) (in French).

## The Aubrac by motorcycle

The Aubrac is a high plateau shared by Aveyron, Lozère and Cantal. Its average altitude is around 1,200 m, and its highest point, the signal de Mailhebiau, reaches 1,469 m ([Wikipédia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monts_d%27Aubrac)). Cattle, mostly of the Aubrac breed, graze up there from 25 May to 13 October; the move up is celebrated on the weekend closest to 25 May, notably in the village of Aubrac ([Tourisme Occitanie](https://www.tourisme-occitanie.com/fr/fiche/fete-et-manifestation/fete-de-la-transhumance-en-aubrac-aubrac_TFOFMALAR0480003798/)). Expect crowds that weekend, and cows on the road all summer.

- **À la découverte de l'Aubrac**: a 91 km loop from Saint-Geniez-d'Olt (1 h 35, 5.3/10), over the Portalet (1,370 m) and the col d'Aubrac (1,340 m). [Route page](/roadbook-moto/occitanie/circuit-decouverte-motorise-a-la-decouverte-de-l-aubrac-au-d/).
- **Vallée du Lot, Aubrac et Truyère**: 244 km from Bozouls to Conques (4 h 20, 6.3/10), the twistiest route in the département, past the Bozouls canyon, the Sarrans dam and the Valon viewpoint. [Route page](/roadbook-moto/occitanie/vallee-du-lot-aubrac-et-truyere/).

> **Watch out.** Snow is possible on the plateau from October to May, and wind-blown drifts can last into late spring. In summer, slow down near cattle and give them all the room they need.
{: .note-warn}

## The Millau Viaduct and the Larzac

The [Millau Viaduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millau_Viaduct) is 2,460 m long and carries the A75 motorway (toll). Riding over it is like any motorway; the best view is from below. The Laguiole–Millau road trip and the *10 Plus Beaux Villages de France* circuit both pass under it along the Tarn valley. From Millau, **[Des gorges de la Dourbie au plateau du Larzac](/roadbook-moto/occitanie/des-gorges-de-la-dourbie-au-plateau-du-larzac/)** (137 km, 2 h 25, 5.3/10) climbs onto the Larzac through La Couvertoirade and Le Caylar.

## Two easy days: villages and bastides

The **[Circuit des 10 Plus Beaux Villages de France](/roadbook-moto/occitanie/circuit-des-10-plus-beaux-villages-de-france/)** links ten villages labelled among France's most beautiful (Belcastel, Conques, Estaing, Saint-Côme-d'Olt, Sainte-Eulalie-d'Olt, Peyre, La Couvertoirade, Brousse-le-Château, Sauveterre-de-Rouergue, Najac) in 450 km over two days. It is the least twisty of the lot (4.1/10): comfortable two-up.

## Practical tips

- **Pick the season by altitude.** The Aubrac is best ridden from late May to early October; the Lot, Aveyron and Tarn valleys also suit spring and autumn.
- **Fill up before the plateaus**: fuel stations are sparse up there.
- **Book your nights** near the stage ends (Entraygues-sur-Truyère, Najac, Rodez, Brousse-le-Château) before you leave.
- **Riding in France?** Keep the Highway Code's rules in mind, and remember that radar warnings are banned: navigation apps may only show "danger zones".
