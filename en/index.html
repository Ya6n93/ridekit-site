The motorcycle app for iPhone

Your ride. One tap. Let's go.

Tell RideKit how long you have and which roads you like: it draws a twisty loop from where you are, guides you by voice and keeps the ride in your journal. With your friends on the map, and a loved one told if you crash.

  • RideKit is coming to the App Store in France first. The app is in French; Android will come later.

  • No account: your rides, your bike and your settings stay on your phone.

What RideKit does

A loop in one tap

Pick a duration (1 to 5 h) and fast or twisty roads; avoid motorways, tolls, dirt roads, ferries or tunnels. 3 loops a month free, unlimited with Pro.

Group rides

The leader shares a code or a QR; everyone sees the others on the map, gets the leader's route and quick messages read aloud. 2 riders free, up to 8 when the leader has Pro.

Crash detection Pro

During a guided ride, after a likely crash and no answer, your emergency contact gets a text with your position (French mobiles). An aid, not a guarantee: it tells a loved one, not the emergency services.

Roadbook

97 road trips in France from open data, stage by stage, each with a page and a free GPX track (in French).

Riding weather

Hourly forecast, the best time slot to ride, live rain radar, mountain pass weather.

Voice navigation

Free turn-by-turn voice guidance, screen locked too, speed limits and French danger zones. With Pro, a Copilot announces hairpins, rain and the last fuel station.

And more

Fuel prices in France, cols and viewpoints on your route, GPX/KML/TCX import (export with Pro), share your ride as an image, garage with maintenance reminders.
