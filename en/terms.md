## 1. What RideKit is

RideKit is a motorcycle riding app. It generates loops from the length and road style
you choose, draws routes from one place to another, guides you, imports and exports
GPX tracks, shows places along the way (passes, sights, fuel stations, cafés), the
weather and rain radar, speed limits and, in France, danger zones. It keeps your ride
journal and your bike's records, and offers crash detection and group rides. Some of
these features are RideKit Pro features (section 7).

These features depend on GPS, the network and third-party services (section 10). They
may change, be added or be removed.

## 2. On the road, you are the one riding

- **The Highway Code, road signs and instructions from the police always prevail**
  over what the app shows or announces.
- A route is a suggestion. It may use a road that is closed, under works, in poor
  condition, unpaved, closed to traffic or unsuited to your bike, your skills or the
  weather. Judge for yourself, and turn back if needed.
- A "twisty" loop is not an invitation to ride fast. Adapt your speed to the road,
  visibility, traffic and weather.
- **Don't handle your phone while riding.** Plan your ride when stopped, put the phone
  on a suitable mount, and only listen to announcements with equipment allowed where
  you ride (for instance a helmet-integrated kit in France).
- You alone are responsible for your riding, your licence, your insurance and your
  bike's condition.

## 3. Data that may be wrong or late

Everything RideKit shows is **for guidance only**: routes, distances, durations, speed
limits, danger zones, weather, radar, fuel prices, places and opening hours,
elevations, maps. This data comes from third-party sources (section 10) and may be
incomplete, inaccurate or outdated: a limit that changed, a wrong forecast, a radar
showing the last hours' images with a delay, a price a station didn't update.

Location and speed come from GPS. They may be imprecise (tunnels, valleys, city
centres); the speed shown may differ from your speedometer and proves nothing in a
roadside check. On some routes (imported tracks, fallback route), the speed limit is
unknown: the app then shows none.

## 4. Danger zones and speed cameras

**In France** (mainland and overseas departments), RideKit announces "danger zones", as
French regulation provides (article R413-15 of the Code de la route): a stretch of road
of at least 300 m in town, 2 km outside built-up areas and 4 km on motorways, **never
giving a camera's location or type**. They are computed from the official list of
fixed speed cameras published by the Ministry of the Interior on data.gouv.fr. This
list may be incomplete or lag behind reality (cameras added, moved or removed), and
contains neither mobile cameras nor police checks: no alert does not mean no check.

**Everywhere else** — Belgium, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Canada and every other
country — RideKit announces **neither danger zones nor cameras**. In some countries,
such as Switzerland, warning of speed checks is prohibited. The app decides by the
road's country: when it is unknown, nothing is announced. Only the overspeed alert
(a spoken line, or a beep with the voice off) remains available, where the limit is
known.

RideKit is not a radar detector. Alerts are turned off in Réglages › Alertes.

## 5. Crash detection

**Crash detection is an aid, not an emergency service.** It replaces neither
emergency services nor a certified alert device. In an emergency, call **112**
(Europe, including Switzerland) or **911** (Canada).

- It is a RideKit Pro feature (section 7): without an active subscription, it does
  not watch your rides, even if turned on before. It is off until you turn it on,
  and only works during a guided ride. Depending on
  the phone and its system, it may stop when the app is no longer on screen or is
  closed.
- A phone alone cannot recognise a crash for sure. It may **miss** a crash (a slow
  fall, the phone separated from the bike or rider, GPS lost, the phone damaged, a flat
  battery, the app closed) or **report one wrongly**.
- When it believes you crashed, an alarm rings, at the phone's volume, and a countdown
  starts. Without an answer, a text with your location goes to your emergency
  contact. RideKit lets your close one know: it **does not alert emergency services**.
- To a French mobile number (the only ones served for now), this text, a single
  one, goes by itself, through RideKit's server and
  Brevo, if the phone has a network (mobile data); without one, the app keeps trying
  for 5 minutes. Otherwise, or if that sending fails, the message is prepared in the
  messaging app and someone has to tap "Send" for it to go. In every case, sending
  depends on the mobile network, the battery, carriers and providers: a text may
  arrive late, or not at all. The server caps the number of texts a day, against
  abuse.
- RideKit doesn't text your contact when you register them: it is up to you to tell
  them, with the text the app prepares for you, sent from your phone. Tell them they
  may receive an alert, check their number, and try the alert with the tests in the
  app; the test sent by RideKit's server ("Tester l'envoi automatique") is a RideKit
  Pro feature.

## 6. Group rides

- Creating a group ride and joining one are **free**. A group takes **2 people**, the
  leader included, or **up to 8** when its leader has RideKit Pro; if they no longer
  have it during the ride, nobody is removed, only newcomers are refused.
- The other members' locations, messages and alerts are **indicative**: they depend
  on their network and GPS, and may be late or missing. "Plus de nouvelles" (no news)
  often means "no network".
- An alert relayed to the group **does not alert emergency services**: in an
  emergency, call **112** (**911** in Canada).
- **Do not handle your phone while riding**: quick messages are picked when stopped.
- For riders aged 15 and over only. What the ride shares, with whom and for how long,
  is described in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).

## 7. RideKit Pro (subscription)

RideKit is free, with limits. RideKit Pro includes:

- **crash detection** (section 5) and, to a French mobile number, the alert sent to
  your close one by itself, with its test ("Tester l'envoi automatique");
- the **voice co-pilot**: dangerous bends, rain, last fuel station, danger zones,
  spoken during guidance;
- **unlimited loops** (without Pro: 3 a month);
- **downloading offline maps** (maps already downloaded remain usable);
- **group rides of up to 8 people** when the leader has RideKit Pro (without: 2,
  section 6);
- **sharing GPX files**;
- **routes of more than 5 points** ("Créer le parcours"; without Pro: 5, start and
  finish included).

Everything else is free, including "Tester la détection", voice guidance, the choice
of roads, importing tracks, sharing a ride as an image and the Roadbook. The details
are described in the app at the time of purchase.

- **Plans and prices**: monthly (€5.99/month) or yearly (€49.99/year), prices
  including VAT in France. The price shown in the app before you confirm the purchase
  prevails.
- **Where to buy it**: on iPhone, through the App Store. RideKit Pro is not sold on
  Google Play yet.
- **Free trial**: the yearly subscription starts with 7 free days if you have never
  used the trial (one trial per App Store account). Unless cancelled at least 24 hours
  before they end, the subscription starts and is charged to your App Store account.
  Any unused portion of a trial is forfeited if you subscribe during it. The monthly
  plan has no trial: it is charged at purchase.
- **Payment**: charged to your App Store account when you confirm the purchase or, for
  the yearly plan with a trial, at the end of the 7 free days.
- **Auto-renewal**: the subscription renews for the same period unless cancelled at
  least 24 hours before the end of the current period; renewal is charged within the
  24 hours before that end.
- **Manage or cancel**: at any time, in your App Store account settings (Réglages ›
  Abonnement, in the app, takes you there). Deleting the app does not cancel the

- **Refunds and withdrawal**: payment is collected by Apple; refund requests and the

- **Price changes**: announced to you under App Store rules, and you can cancel before

- **Restore purchases**: on a new phone, with the same App Store account ("Restaurer
